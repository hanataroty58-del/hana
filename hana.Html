The Vision: Sustainable Living Beyond Earth

The 'Home in Space' project is the answer to environmental challenges and population growth on Earth. We are building the first self-sufficient orbital colony, designed to provide an Earth-like living environment but with unprecedented energy efficiency.

Our station relies on orbital solar power and a closed-loop system for water and resource recycling, ensuring every resident enjoys the highest standards of quality and comfort. This is not just a station; it is a complete community in the heart of the cosmos.

Orbital Sustainability Index

  • Energy Efficiency: 99.8%
  • Water Recycling: 100%
  • Green Area per Capita: 50 m²

Unparalleled Features

Artificial Gravity
